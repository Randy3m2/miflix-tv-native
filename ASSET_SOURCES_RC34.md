# Franchise logo assets · RC34

Bundled PNGs are rasterizations of the logo artwork from these sources, not font recreations:

- `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/franchise_dc.png`: DC official website asset, https://static.dc.com/2024-08/dc-logo-313x313.svg (linked by https://www.dc.com/).
- `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/franchise_marvel.png`: Marvel logo artwork hosted at https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b9/Marvel_Logo.svg ; source record https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Marvel_Logo.svg (PD-textlogo record).

Original logos/trademarks belong to their respective owners. Used to identify the existing franchise collections; no affiliation is represented. Source retrieved October 12, 2026. SVGs were converted to 800px-wide PNGs and displayed with aspect ratio preserved.
